What is OcuScan?
Your personal eye screening tool — in your pocket.

OcuScan uses your phone or laptop camera to check how well your eyes are working — without any special equipment, doctor visits, or expensive tests. In just a few minutes, it gives you a clear picture of your vision health.

👓
Do you need glasses?
OcuScan checks how sharp your vision is at a distance — the same way a doctor would with a standard eye chart, but done right from your screen.
📖
Near vision check
Checks how well you read up close — useful for spotting reading strain or early presbyopia.
🌫
Contrast sensitivity
Tests your ability to see low-contrast letters — can catch early signs of eye conditions that a standard chart misses.
📄
Get a real report
At the end, download a clean PDF report you can show your eye doctor — with your results, estimated power, and personalised advice.
⚠️ OcuScan is a screening tool, not a medical diagnosis. Always see a qualified eye doctor before buying glasses or making any health decisions.

Before You Begin

Get your space ready.

Turn your brightness up, dim the room, and sit about 50–70 cm from the screen.

Set your iris diameter

Contact lens prescriptions sometimes list iris/corneal diameter (typically 11.0–13.0 mm). Setting this reduces Snellen distance error from ±11% to near zero.
