The process

Three steps. Six minutes. One clear answer.

01

Calibrate

Hold a card-sized object to the screen — or let auto-detect handle it — so every measurement is scaled to real-world size.

02

Screen

Your camera quietly tracks distance and eye position while you complete four short, guided tests.

03

Review

Get a plain-English summary of each result, and a clear read on whether it's time to book an eye exam.

What we measure

Four tests, one sitting.

Visual Acuity

The Snellen letter test, reimagined to auto-scale as your distance from the screen changes.

Near Vision

A close-range gap-orientation test — the same skill you're using to read this sentence.

Contrast Sensitivity

Finds the point where low-contrast letters start to fade — often the earliest sign something's off.

Refractive Estimate

A two-phase blur test that estimates near- or far-sightedness, and by roughly how much.

Why OcuScan

Built to inform your next step — not replace your eye doctor.

Every test question is generated and scored on the server, so results can't be gamed by the client. Your camera feed never leaves your browser — it's used only to track distance and eye position in real time. OcuScan Pro is a screening tool, not a diagnosis: treat any concerning result as a reason to book a full exam with a licensed optometrist.
